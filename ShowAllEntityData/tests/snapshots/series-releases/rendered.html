▲Annotation

La Repubblica is an Italian daily newspaper and L’Espresso is an Italian weekly news magazine. Between November 2008 and February 2009, La Repubblica and L’Espresso ran a reissue series consisting of Bruce Springsteen’s first twelve studio albums. The titles were sold with the newspaper or the magazine and were also available for purchase separately at newsstands. The albums, which were each housed in 5.4″x5.4″ mini‐LP sleeve, were specially pressed for this series and feature unique catalogue numbers with “BSC” prefixes. They originally came sealed with a cardboard display card. The first title in the series, Born In The U.S.A., was sealed on the display card together with a folded exclusive cardboard slipcase to store the complete series.

The 12 releases in the series are:
‐ Born in the U.S.A. (CD), released on 26 Nov 2008
‐ Born to Run (CD), released on 03 Dec 2008
‐ The Rising (CD), released on 10 Dec 2008
‐ The River (2xCD), released on 17 Dec 2008
‐ Tunnel of Love (CD), released on 24 Dec 2008
‐ Nebraska (CD), released on 31 Dec 2008
‐ Greetings From Asbury Park, N.J. (CD), released on 07 Jan 2009
‐ The Ghost of Tom Joad (CD), released on 14 Jan 2009
‐ The Wild, the Innocent and the E Street Shuffle (CD), released on 21 Jan 2009
‐ Darkness on the Edge Of Town (CD), released on 28 Jan 2009
‐ Lucky Town (CD), released on 04 Feb 2009
‐ Human Touch (CD), released on 11 Feb 2009
