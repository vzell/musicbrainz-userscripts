Brucebase event windows

The window and hover card of a Brucebase event page (a gig, a recording session, an interview) on any site. Today the window leaves out three things the page has: the description above the tabs, a cast tab that is not On Stage (In Studio, On Set, On Site, …) and an interview's Playlist. It also leaves out the venue's other lists (Appearances, Cancelled, …) and an older page's News tab. This mockup shows five real pages from the wiki, today and with batch 1. What batch 1 adds is green.

Questions

2. Does the hover card show the description's first sentence?

3. A cast tab that is not On Stage (In Studio, On Set, On Site, …): how is its bold line shown (“Greetings From Asbury Park, N.J. Recording Session”)?

4. Should the year table's On stage column (Phase 14) also show a recording's In Studio people?

5. The venue's other lists (Appearances, Cancelled, Performances (w/o Bruce), From My Home To Yours)?

6. An older page's “News” tab (77 of the pages sampled), which is the News/Memorabilia tab under its old name?

Not a question: the Playlist reads like the Setlist. The facts get “Playlist: 11 songs: …”, and the section numbers the songs, each with its original artist, the song names in MusicBrainz's title style as the Setlist does. Its note (“Bruce did not perform…”) comes last.

What the sample shows: the description is on nearly every event page (228 of 233 gigs, all 53 nogig pages, 89 of 91 recordings, all 44 interviews); In Studio is on 95 pages, Playlist on 10, the other lists on 300. Nothing new is requested: all of it is in the page the window already reads.
