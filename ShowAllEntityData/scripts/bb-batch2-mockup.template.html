Brucebase person and release windows

The window and hover card of a Brucebase person page and of a release or playlist page, on any site. Today a person's window leaves out the biography above the tabs and its Wikipedia source. A release's window leaves out its On The Tracks versions, its Recording 'Live' Dates and its Gallery/News press links; the release's own page view already reads the first two. This mockup shows five real pages, today and with batch 2. What batch 2 adds is green; what question 6 asks about is marked “asked”.
