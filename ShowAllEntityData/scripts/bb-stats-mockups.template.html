SURVEY

What the mirror holds

1,942 stats: pages: 1,299 are one song's tour counts, 258 are album studio-session pages and their parts, 86 are award fragments. The rest are about 300 pages of their own. The survey read every page in the copy below; the tour family was sampled (one in 50) and gets a full run before its specs.

A

Tabbed link lists

Batch 1 proposed

The largest group, : tabs (letters as on stats:songs, or names: labels, years, tours), each tab a list of links to one kind of page. One reader covers them all. A show link's text, "2014-12-07 Kennedy Center, Washington, DC", splits into Date, Venue, City and State. The wiki's sort form "Left Foot (The)" is turned round. Nested tabs (Audio › Born To Run (1975)) become one path.

B

Wiki tables

Batch 1 proposed

hold real tables with a header row: awards per ceremony, song counts per tour, performance counts per city, state and country, the On The Tracks timeline and key, the live top 100, openers and closers. The header becomes the columns. A page with one table per tab or heading gets a sub-table each; numbers sort as numbers.

C

One song's tour counts

Batch 2

1,299 pages stats:<song>-tour, each a run of one-row boxes: "Performed at 59 show(s) of 130 on the 2023-25 International Tour". As a table: Tour, Played, Tour shows and the share. The song page (bb-song) already has "Performances by tour" counted from its own list; this page adds the tour's total.

D

An album's studio sessions

Batch 2

35 main pages stats:<album>-studio-sessions, put together from parts the wiki includes (-overview, -release, -addrec, -details, -credits, -altseq-N, …). Laid out like a release (bb-retail): the head, then a sub-table per part. Each version heading "BORN TO RUN - V1a" splits into Song / Version / Take / Date, the same split the open TODO asks for on On The Tracks.

E

Galleries

Batch 3

The discography pages are cover tiles, each linking a release. As a table: Release, Cover, Tab, with the cover in the image card and the viewer as on other Brucebase pages. What the filename says (disco_<name>) comes from _bbParseImageName(). Below: the tiles as they are read, the pictures drawn as placeholders here.

F

Text pages

Batch 3

stats:songs-registered lists songs registered at the Library of Congress as plain text, no links. As a table with a Song page column, matched by title against stats:songs: . The registration record above the list stays as text above the table.
