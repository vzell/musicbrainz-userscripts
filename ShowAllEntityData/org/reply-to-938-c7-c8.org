Hi Miodrag - @majkinetor,

thanks for points 7 and 8, and for listing SAED as complementary. Both are in SAED's development version now
(`main` of vzell/musicbrainz-userscripts: 9.99.1643 and 9.99.1646), checked with String Theory from your `main`.

**7. `mbLastWrite`.** SAED reads it. Its link previews (entity cards, a release group's releases and facts, edit and
editor pages, the paged lists in its windows) treat an answer kept at or before that time as stale, in memory and
in IndexedDB, so after a rating or a tag vote in Event Horizon, SAED's next card or window asks again. SAED writes nothing to
MusicBrainz, so it never sets the key. Its long-lived caches (the Relationships column's, 30 days) don't follow it,
because none of the writes Event Horizon reports can change relationships.

**8. `MBU.artistImage`.** Looked up at call time, artists and labels only:
- an artist's or a label's window (opened with Space) calls `findAll()` and shows your pictures after SAED's own
  (MusicBrainz's image and logo links, else Wikidata), in the user's Event Horizon order, marked "via Event Horizon".
  Your Wikipedia picture is left out when SAED already shows a Commons file, since it's usually the same photo;
- the hover card and the picture beside an artist's or label's name use `cached()` only, so a hover or a page load
  asks nobody;
- a throw, a reject or no answer within 20 s leaves SAED's own pictures, with nothing said; a setting turns it
  off.
