1.)

My own convention for disambiguation comments is that they should follow the naming conventions from the style guide at
https://musicbrainz.org/doc/Style/Recording#Live_recordings - Specific types of recording: Live recordings

#+begin_example
For live recordings, enter the recording title as described in the recording title guideline, and use "live" as the recording disambiguation comment. If the date and/or location is known, this should also be added to the disambiguation comment following the same format as used on live bootlegs.

Examples
Train in Vain (live, 1998-12-14: Telewest Arena, Newcastle, England, UK)
Wake Up (live, Los Angeles, CA, USA)
The Dance (live, 2002)
Candle in the Wind (single edit) (live)
#+end_example

But personally, at least for artist Bruce Springsteen, I use unicode "‐" between all date components but plain "-" or a
mixture of both are allowed (the latter are flagged as warnings)

Additionally I allow also "; <event additional info>" at the end of the disambiguation, so basically

#+begin_example
<event type>, YYYY-MM-DD[, early show]: Venue, City, State, Country[; <event additional info>]
#+end_example
