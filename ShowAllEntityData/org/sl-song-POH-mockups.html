The springsteenlyrics.com song window

Proposals for the preview window you get with Space on a lyrics.php?song=… link. Every tab is a working mock of the window: sections open and close (Ctrl+click toggles all), tables filter and sort, the lyrics compare with the album version. The last tab collects your picks as text to paste back.

Data from debug/sl-song-*-POH.html (Backstreets: album, unofficial studio 1, live 18 Nov 1975) and five more pages surveyed 2026-11-07. Lyrics are placeholders written for this page.

Lyrics face

1Sections fold

Every heading is a fold. Lyrics, Info and the release tracklists start open. The long prose (release story, Other Official Releases, Sad Eyes, Backstreets Magazine) starts closed. Each heading carries a size hint, e.g. "28 releases" or "61". Ctrl+click on a heading opens or closes all of them, as on Brucebase. A fold you change stays that way for the page's life.

2A lyric sheet

The lyrics get their own block: a book face at 15 px with generous leading, a paper tone, and real stanza gaps. Stage notes such as [Spoken:] are set in grey italic small caps. A run of identical lines folds to one line with a "× 9" badge, and a click unfolds it. Copy copies the plain text.

Faces: the system book serif needs no download. EB Garamond and Literata are Google web fonts, so the userscript would have to load them from fonts.googleapis.com.

4Two columns, balanced

Left: what the version is. The title block holds a type pill and the version navigator. Small cards show Version, On the site and Releases, with a bar of the 61 versions by type. Then Info, the studio facts (dates, studio, takes, the band as name → instruments), images, Credits and Request.

Right: what you read. A row of section chips (7), the lyrics, then the releases and lists.

35Real tracklists, real rows

A release's track lists are drawn from the editions the reader already has, side by side, with this song ● and every other song a link that opens in the window. Other releases, covers and movies become rows with a thumbnail instead of a text dump. ALL CAPS song names in prose (BORN TO RUN) are linked when the site's song list knows them.

6Version navigator

‹ 1 of 61 › steps through this song's versions in the site's order and stays in the window. The ‹ › in the window bar keep stepping through the table rows, as today.

11Prose polish

Quotes from interviews and liner notes become pull quotes. "PAGE UNDER CONSTRUCTION" and "SECTION NOT YET COMPLETED" are dropped. "Page last updated" moves to the foot.

9Differs from the album version in scope

On any version other than the album version, the window reads the album version's page as well. The reference is the version marked Album; failing that, the first official studio version; failing that, there is no comparison. The read uses the same cache and rate gate and starts when the window opens, never on a hover.

  • Marks: ~ changed lines with the changed words picked out (hover one for the album line), + added lines, and optionally − missing album lines.
  • Side by side: both sheets, aligned by stanza.
  • The chip counts the lines: "17 of 46 lines differ".
  • Case, punctuation and spacing are ignored, as are stage notes and blank lines.

The marks in this mock are computed live by the same line-by-line algorithm the script would use.

Left column on a thin page

An unofficial version has little beyond its lyrics. The left column then carries the facts (Recorded, Circulates on: War And Roses), the Info text and Credits, and the right column is all lyrics.

A live version

  • The Show card replaces the bare fields: date with weekday, venue, place, tour, and Released on.
  • The show's tracklist becomes a table (proposal 12). This song is ●, every other song links its page, and the MB work glyphs show as today.
  • The lyrics open in Marks mode, so the ad-libs and changed lines stand out against the album version.
  • The long release story and the liner notes fold closed. The liner notes become a pull quote.

12Filterable, sortable tables your ask

These tables use the window's own List table, the one the MusicBrainz and Discogs windows already have: a filter, three-state sort on every column (the third click restores the site's order), and chips by Type. While the table is unsorted, a heading row opens each type.

  • Available versions: Title · Version · Type · Date · Year. This version is ●. A title opens its page in this window, and Back returns here.
  • Release tracklists: one table per edition, with the disc or record as a part.
  • Live tracklist, Live History dates (Tour chips), releases, covers, singles (40 px thumbnails).

Try the filter ("1978", "unofficial"), a chip, or a header.

7Section chips

The chips above the right column jump to a section and open it. The row stays at the top while you scroll.

4Variant B: the lyrics on the left
The left column holds the title block and the lyric sheet alone, a song-sheet reading. The right column holds everything else: the fact cards, Info, the releases, the lists. The columns are 1 : 1.
This suits a reader who comes for the words. Variant A (tab A) suits a reader who comes for the facts.
