*** 1.) Why archive.org plays these bootlegs

The items are uploads by archive.org users, not the Archive's own releases. The 1975 shows sit in
user collections (=radionowhere=, =roiocollection=) inside "Community Audio" (=folksoundomy=); the
four 1975 videos in "Community Video" (=opensource_movies=). The Internet Archive does not check
rights before an upload is published. As a US host of user content it relies on the DMCA's
notice-and-takedown rule (17 U.S.C. § 512): it is not liable as long as it removes an item when a
rights holder sends a valid notice. So an unofficial recording stays playable until someone with
the rights asks for it to be taken down. Many uploads have been removed that way over the years.
This differs from the Live Music Archive (=etree=), which only takes bands that allow taping and
trading; as far as I know Springsteen is not one of them, which is why these items are in the
community collections instead. (Not legal advice; this describes how the site works.)

*** The search page has no rows in its HTML (2.)

=debug/ia-1975-tile-view-initial-.html= and =debug/ia-1975-compact-list-view-initial.html= are
byte-identical, 10.5 KB: an =<app-root>= Lit web-component shell. The results, and the Tile /
List / Compact list views, are drawn by the page's scripts into shadow DOM. So "scrape the
Compact list view" has nothing to scrape; the rows come from the Internet Archive's search API.

- Q10: a track link opens its show's window with that row marked.
- Not answered, so the mockup's recommendation stands until said otherwise: Q2 all media types
  with a Type chip each; Q3 the shaded columns as shown; Q4 first sorted by date; Q8 description
  clamped with ▾; Q9 a strip of the first 12 thumbnails, Space for all.

Embed probe (=scripts/probe-ia-embed.py=, 2026-10-31 before 19:21+01:00, vzell-lap): =archive.org/embed/<id>=
and =/embed/<id>/<file>= answer 200 with no X-Frame-Options and no CSP =frame-ancestors=, so
another site may frame them. MusicBrainz's own CSP =frame-src= could still refuse the frame; not
checkable from vzell-lap (MetaBrainz resets IPv4 here). The YouTube player's fallback (the page
refuses the frame → the tile opens the site in a new tab, =securitypolicyviolation=) covers that.

   MusicBrainz detection; =@match=/=@include= for =https://archive.org/search*=, =@connect archive.org=.
2. *pageType =ia-search=* (any search page): =features.pageRequest= with =fetchText()= to
   =advancedsearch.php= — the page's =query=, =and[]= filters and =sort= translated to Solr; 100
   rows a request, the count from =numFound=, one request a second. Converter =_iaToHtml()= →
   =table.tbl=: Art (lazy, =archive.org/services/img/<id>=, as Jungleland's Art column), Date,
   Title (the item), Venue, Source, Type, Views, Favorites, Reviews, Added, Size, Collection,
   Topics, Description (prose collapse). First sort: Date. Placement on the live page: the page is
   an =<app-root>= web-component shell, so our section goes outside it (like =_dgPrepareLivePage()=
   keeps ours outside =#app=); to be checked on a saved FRP.
3. *Reader =iadetails=* in =_EXT_READERS= (any host): =archive.org/details/<id>[/<file>]=,
   =keyUrl= = =/details/<id>= (a track link shares its show's request); =load()= = one anonymous
   =_extGet()= of =/metadata/<id>=, trimmed to what the card and window use. Card: kind, title,
   source/tracks/runtime pills, date, venue, collection, views, first songs (a track link: the
   track and its place). Window: left facts, description, links (Brucebase/Databruce open in the
