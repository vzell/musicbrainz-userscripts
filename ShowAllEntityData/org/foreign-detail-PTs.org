** findings

*** release-tracks, the model

- Top to bottom:
  1. the h1 toolbar (Tracks · Credits);
  2. "Cover art (N)" (chips, Grid / By type / Spreads, the viewer);
  3. =h2.tracklist= with the global filter;
  4. one h3 + =table.tbl= per medium ("1 - CD", collapsible, medium art);
  5. the "Sidebar" cards.
- Columns: # · Title · Artist · Length (⏱) · …, then the AR columns: Recording of work, Recorded at …, Performer,
  Vocals, Instruments, Producer, Engineer, Mixer, ℗ …, plus dynamic ones. They are =collapsableColumns=, multi-row.
- The Cover-art section, the medium art and the Sidebar cards are tied to MusicBrainz:
  - =_sbInlineInsertSection()= returns on any =_foreignHost=;
  - =_artSectionDesc()= is keyed on the pageType.
- A foreign detail page therefore needs those generalised behind a provider, or equivalents of its own.

*** Discogs release: =dg-release= exists (upgrade, not a new PT)

- One API request plus the master's versions.
- Sub-tables today:
  - Tracklist (Pos, Title, Duration, one Credits cell per track);
  - Credits (Role, Name, Tracks);
  - Companies;
  - Identifiers;
  - Videos;
  - Other versions.
- Missing compared with release-tracks:
  - no sub-table per side or disc: positions stay "A1", heading rows are dropped;
  - no track artists;
  - Duration is plain text: no duration sort, no ⏱;
  - credits are not split into role columns, and the Credits table's "Tracks" ranges are not mapped onto tracks;
  - no images section for the release's own images (the data is in the answer);
  - no facts box (format, label, country, genres, notes, have/want);
  - no MusicBrainz column on the Tracklist.

*** archive.org item: new PT =ia-details=

- =/details/<id>= is not =@include='d today; only the search page is.
- The item page is server-rendered, NOT a web-component shell. Audio items embed the whole metadata JSON in
  =input.js-ia-metadata= (seen in browser saves; unconfirmed for the raw server HTML).
- One anonymous =/metadata/<id>= request (no key) has everything: the request the =iadetails= card already makes and
  caches.
- Per file: name, format, source (original or derivative), original, size, md5, length, title, track.
- The current parse (=_extIaItem()=) drops track, size, md5 and the derivative relation, so a richer parse is needed
  (bump =_EXT_IA_VERSION=).
- Reusable: the pictures (=_IA_FI=, the viewer), the ▶ archive.org player (=_yt*=, source =ia=), and the Brucebase
  and Databruce links.
