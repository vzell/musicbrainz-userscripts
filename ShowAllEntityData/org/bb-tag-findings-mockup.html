What is built if you approve

  • A missing tag is drawn as a red dashed chip in its family's row. It is not counted in “Tags (N)”: that number stays the tags the wiki has.
  • A tag that is not expected keeps its place and gets an amber outline.
  • Two pills in the heading give the counts. A press on one, or “Findings” in the Show control, leaves only the marked tags.
  • Under the tags each finding is said in words, with what made the tag expected.
  • Nothing is marked before every page that keeps the show's tags is read, so a tag kept on the On stage page is never called missing.
  • The year table's Tags cell lists every tag, a row each, foldable. Tags from the On stage page carry ↪. Its findings are in the ❌ and ⚠️ menus, the column's 📊 and the cell's hover card.
