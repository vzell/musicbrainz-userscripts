**** GPE and FICE: not the same thing

- *GPE* (generic popup engine) previews *any link*: the hover card =#mb-dp-peek= and the Space
  window =#mb-dp-dialog= (=_popSources()=, =_DP_SITES=, the =_ext*= readers). Pictures are one
  thing its window can show (D).
- *FICE* (foreign image card engine) is the picture card and viewer click for *table rows* on
  the foreign hosts (C): its card is drawn in the stat tooltip =#mb-stat-tooltip= through
  =_mbttResolve()= (=fi-img=), its click is its own window-capture listener (=_fiClick()=).
- What they share is *below and after* the UI: the detail-page reader (=_dpGet()=, =_dpMem=,
  its own IndexedDB, the per-site rate gates) — a row the link preview already read costs FICE
  nothing — and the viewer. Where they meet: a FICE trigger inside a GPE window (Discogs window
  tables, =_DGW_FI=) gets the window's ring, but its card, click and Space are FICE's.
- B is a third path again: the head's =fd-pic= card is deliberately not FICE, because FICE is
  shaped around rows (=rowFor()=, row stepping). Diagram in the mockup ("GPE vs FICE").
