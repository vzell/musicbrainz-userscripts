KillingFloor record pages and their previews

A record's own page (DB/detrec.aspx?code=…) laid out like a MusicBrainz release: a sub-table per record or disc, the show each track comes from split into Date, Show and Country, and the record's facts, notes, sources and photo above. The song and show links on the page, today dead href="#" script links, get hover cards and a window. Every value below comes from the five snapshots and the probed song and show pages.

Record page new PT bs-record

Runs in its own tab and in the frameset's right frame. One button, "Tracklist". No request: the rows come from the page itself.

Song and show previews 2 new kinds

sd3.aspx?sid= (the song's lyric versions) and mn.aspx?ex= (the show's setlist and the records it is on). One request each, on hover or Space, under the existing KF preview setting.

Reader fix 1 lost line

"The original version is numbered." on All Those Years (LP) sits between the facts and the Notes, and the current reader drops it. It becomes a note.

Record:

Notes on this view

  • The site's banner, nav links and notice stay as they are. Our section starts after them, so the button sits under the site's own header.
  • Sub-tables follow decision 3. 10 LP with 20 groups gives Records 1–10 with the sides paired. 2 LP with 2 groups gives Records 1–2. 1 7″ with 2 groups gives Record 1 (sides A + B). CDs give one disc per group. Pos keeps the site's "3/2".
  • Date / Show / Country come from the From cell, and a ditto (") repeats the row above. Show links to mn.aspx?ex=… (the code keeps its "/1" show index) and previews.
  • Title is in MusicBrainz title style (sa_foreign_mb_titles, on by default), with the site's spelling in the tooltip. It links to the song page and previews.
  • Duplicate positions (CD 1/2, 4/1, 5/16) are medleys and stay as two rows.
  • Sources, on the right, lists every show the tracks come from with its track count, in tracklist order.
  • Photo is shown only when the site's image loaded. The site hides a missing one, as on The River, 30 Tracks and the CD box.

Notes on this view

  • Before any button: the site's page untouched, plus our h1 with the Tracklist button.
  • Each song and show link is href="#" with an onclick. Its href becomes the real page (sd3.aspx?sid=586, mn.aspx?ex=S198010101), so the hover card and Space work on it. The onclick stays, so a plain click still opens the site's own small window, as today.
  • Without that rewrite, # points at the record page itself, which the record reader would preview: every song would show this record.
  • The status line shows what the browser now reports for the hovered link.

Notes on this view

  • The card: the song, its number of versions, and the opening lines of the first version (as springsteenlyrics.com's song card does).
  • The window (Space): one section per version, with the take's name, its lyrics (folded after eight lines) and its note. Facts on the right.
  • The site's "Statistics from the KILLING FLOOR database" footer links nowhere useful and is left out.
  • One request: sd3.aspx?sid=586, cached under the key without hecan=1, half a year like the record reader.
Show:

Notes on this view

  • The card: date, venue, country, the number of songs and the first few.
  • The window: the setlist, with a "[…]" remark as a dim note, the show's Notes, and "Found on": the records the site lists for the show, each with its format from the site's icon (supp8.gif = CD-R, supp9.gif = DVD, …).
  • A record in "Found on" opens in the same window, with Back. That is the existing record reader, so it shows a tracklist and the record's facts.
  • An album-release "show" (code starting with S) writes every line as "Version 99 - Song [Released version]". That becomes the song alone, numbered, with the kind "Release / studio".

Plan

  1. A new pageType bs-record on DB/detrec.aspx?code=: an @include line, and the frame gate opened for the right frame (principale) only.
  2. No request: the page's own HTML is the one page, read with the existing record reader so the hover card and the page agree.
  3. The head: Notes, Details (facts left, Sources right), and the Photo as the shared art section (chip "Photo", ★ main image, viewer).
  4. Song and show pages become two more kinds of the KF reader, which also works on the other sites wherever such a link appears.
  5. Specs on the five snapshots and three probed pages, a mutation list, docs, the coverage registry and WIP changelog entries. No version bump on the branch.

Questions

1. Ver column.On official records every track says 99, which the show page explains as "Released version". Show the number as the site writes it, or write "Released" for 99?Recommendation: keep the number, and explain 99 in the header's tooltip.
2. Sources card.Keep the derived list of shows on the right?Recommendation: yes. On a compilation like All Those Years (30 shows) it is the quickest overview.
3. Photo type.The site's one picture is a composed photo of the item. Label its chip "Photo", or leave it untyped?Recommendation: "Photo", the word the list page's column already uses.
4. Click on the initial page.A plain click on a song or show link keeps opening the site's own small window. Or should it open our window?Recommendation: keep the site's behaviour; Space opens ours.
5. Play column.The YouTube-search tile per track, as on the other detail pages (only with sa_yt_play_in_place on)?Recommendation: yes, query "Bruce Springsteen <song> <date>".

Not seen in any snapshot
