Hi @majkinetor,

as promised, a short report: SAED now books its requests in your shared request gate (`mbu:mb-gate`, protocol v1).
It's on my `main` as 9.99.1583:
https://github.com/vzell/musicbrainz-userscripts/raw/main/ShowAllEntityData/ShowAllEntityData.user.js

What SAED does now, on musicbrainz.org only:

- Every same-origin `/ws/2` request books a slot (`tat`/`cool`, the burst of 3, 1 s gap), *in addition to* SAED's own
  per-tab pacing (1.1 s). Whichever comes later wins, so SAED is never faster than before.
- It reports its answers: 429/503 step `hot` once per episode and set `cool` as in `mbnAnswer`; other answers below 500 decay
  `hot`; 502/504 stay a local retry.
- Background requests wait out another script's `lead`. Explicit clicks book foreground. SAED doesn't take the lead
  itself yet, and there's no browser-check iframe.
- `version: 1` is stamped and unknown fields are kept. An explicit unknown version is left untouched, and SAED falls
  back to its own pacing.
- There's a setting to turn it off ("Share MusicBrainz's request budget with other userscripts", on by default).

The code is ported from your `dev/net/mb-gate.mjs` (main 7eb7c563), with credit in the header. To make that clean,
I relicensed SAED from MIT to **GPL-3.0-or-later**.

Tested: fixture tests for each protocol rule (holds, version handling, lead, two tabs sharing one schedule), plus a
live check in Vivaldi + Tampermonkey with String Theory 2026.11.08.235934: a 20 s `cool` written into
`localStorage['mbu:mb-gate']` held SAED's request for the full 20 s.

One question, no hurry: SAED's big loads are mostly *HTML page* fetches (31 pages of an artist's releases),
not `/ws/2`, and the protocol gates `/ws/2` only. Do you know whether MusicBrainz counts page fetches against the
same per-IP budget? If so, booking them as background might be worth a convention.

And a small reminder of the open point above: keeping V working under `data-mbu-keys-off`.

Thanks!
