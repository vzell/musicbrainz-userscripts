 D. Release-group strip regression: candidate root causes (not yet proven)

 The relevant differences between the two pages:
 - artist-events is tableMode: 'single', EAA, tooltipColumns with Location/Cancelled.
 - releasegroup-releases is tableMode: 'multi', CAA, with stickyColumn: 'Release', an injected Relationships column, renderMultiRowCell Label/Catalog#, and a Country/Date special branch.

 Candidates, most likely first:
 1. Something now covers the strip and catches the pointer (no mouseenter at all). The prime suspect is Sticky Page Headers (_sph*, new 2026-10-02/03). Its pinned wrappers and section bodies only exist on multi-table pages with h3 bars; the events page has none. Check with
    document.elementFromPoint() over a strip image.
 2. The listeners are lost. A multi-table-only path rebuilds, moves or clones the strip's <a> wrappers without going through _artInitBigPics(). The _staleBox block near _ctx.boxPrefix + '-' + _tblIdx (≈63660) and the sticky re-parenting both qualify. Clones lose
    addEventListener.
 3. The handler throws before _tip.style.display = 'block', on a release-group column shape: _artTooltipCountryDate, a multi-row Label/Catalog# cell, or the Relationships cell. This would show as a pageerror on hover.
 4. The tooltip renders but cannot be seen. Its z-index (99998) may sit below a sticky layer, or it may be positioned off-screen.
 5. A third-party clash on the live page only (the jesus2099 bigbox cleanup, INLINE STUFF).

  2. Why the strip tooltip disappeared

  On release-group pages the tooltip includes a Country/Date line. "Right Side Flags Everywhere" wraps the country link in an extra element (span.mfe-flag-wrapper). The code that copies the flag into the tooltip (_artTooltipCountryDate()) assumed the link sat directly inside the
  flag, so it threw "not a child of this node" before the tooltip was shown. The events page has no Country/Date line, which is why it kept working.

  The fix is two lines in ShowAllEntityData.user.js (_artTooltipCountryDate()). It also stops the MusicBrainz flag being drawn next to that script's own flag in the tooltip. It applies to every page that shows Country/Date, for both the strip and the inline thumbnails.

  Verification:
  - The new regression test failed before the fix with the same error as the live page, and passes after.
  - A re-run on the live page with all five of your registered userscripts shows the full tooltip.
  - The full suite: 916 passed, 1 failed. That failure was in my own new events test, which hovered a 1 px image and could miss it. Fixed, and it now passes 15 repeated runs in parallel.
  - Planted-defect checks: 7 of 7 caught.
