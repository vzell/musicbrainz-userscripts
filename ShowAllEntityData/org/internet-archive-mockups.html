Internet Archive in SAED

A new site for the script, like Brucebase and Discogs: its own switch, on by default. A year's search page gets the consolidated table, and every archive.org/details/… link (on archive.org, MusicBrainz, Brucebase, …) gets the hover card and the Space window. Rows come from the Internet Archive's search API, because the search page is drawn by scripts and holds no rows of its own (its Tile and Compact list views are the same saved HTML).
