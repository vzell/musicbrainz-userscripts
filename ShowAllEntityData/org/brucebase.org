  "Info & Setlist" (=/YYYY#ddmmyy[a]=) | "Venue" (=/venue:=) — bare =<br/>= on nogig; gignote

  Mastered/Design/Photography by), footnotes, Released tabview (one tracklist table per edition:
  # | Song Title | Running Time | Release), Additional Information tabview.
- *bootleg*: Label / Catalog # / Time / Format / Source in one =<p>=, =ol= tracklist ("//" = side
  break), Notes, Artwork tab (=Bootleg_<T>_{Front,Back,Disc}.jpg=).
- *Content fragments* (gignote, news, recnote, gallery, media, story, eye) are pulled into the gig's
  tabs; their breadcrumb is the parent gig. onstage/offstage are redirect stubs whose TAGS are the cast.
- *Tags*: =div.page-tags > span > a[href="/system:page-tags/tag/<enc>#pages"]=; families: date parts,
  first letter, category, squashed name, place, material (ticket, bootleg, cancelled, …), prefixed
  (=tour_=, =r_=, =ott_=, =lbl_=, =pl_=, =key_=, =toursnip_=), song abbreviations, dotted person aliases.
- *Image filenames* (14,032 unique sampled; grammar covers 98.5 %):
  A =YYYYMMDD[a]_<KIND|Photographer>_<NN>[a][_detail]= (72 %; KIND Ticket, Setlist, Pass, Centermic,
  Article, AdPoster, Merchandise, …; =Unknown= = unknown photographer; detail Handwritten/Printed,
  Before/After, Backstage, Shirt/Tourbook, a hyphenated subject), B =Retail-CODE_KIND_NN=,
  C =YYYY-TOUR_KIND_NN=, D =disco_|digital_<name>[_variant]=, E =bs<YYMMDD>=, G =<slug>_NN[_date|subject]=,
  F =lyricsheet_|note_|recording_|graph_=, H =Bootleg_<T>_{Front,Back,Disc}=.
