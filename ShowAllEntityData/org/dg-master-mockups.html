What the Tracks button does

  • One request: masters/26725. Today's Versions button costs 1 + 4 for this master.
  • The head, the media split and the credit columns are the release's Tracks view, shared code.
  • A master has no labels, formats or identifiers. So the Master release card holds the year, the main and the newest release.
  • A master has no release-wide credits, so there is no Release credits table. Its producers are on its releases.

Where the numbers come from

  • The master's API answer has no have, want or ratings, only num_for_sale and lowest_price. The Community card reads Discogs' Statistics and For Sale boxes from the page that is already open. That costs no request, but on a disk load the card has only what the answer had.
  • The tracks have no format, so the table is named by the positions: sides make a "Record", "1-1" makes a "Disc".

New on this page new

  • The yellow-ringed cards: Community in two columns, and Credits by person (question 1).
  • Videos as a sub-table under the tracks, with ▶ Play here (question 4).

The change

  • A card of the shared head can say "two columns": the first half of its rows on the left, the rest on the right. The master's Community card uses it too.
  • The two halves are grouped by meaning, so Data quality moves up to the left column (question 3).
  • Numbers get thousands separators, as Discogs' own box writes them.
  • A row the page does not have (no sales yet) is left out; the halves then stay as grouped and need not be even.

Plan

  1. dg-master gets two buttons. "Tracks" first (dgView: 'tracks'), then today's "Versions". The page becomes tableMode: 'multi' with groupByH3, as dg-release. The Versions rows stay as they are.
  2. One request for Tracks. _dgPageCount() returns 1 for a master's Tracks view and keeps the answer. _dgToHtml() page 1 runs the master's answer through _dgReleaseTracksHtml(): the same media split and role columns. No formats means a medium is named by its positions. No release credits means no Release credits table. Then the Videos sub-table, _dgVideosTableHtml().
  3. The head. A _dgMasterHeadSpec() beside _dgReleaseHeadSpec(), through the shared _fdHeadInsertSections(): the byline, Notes, Details (Master release, External links, Genres and styles, Community; Credits by person on the right), Images.
  4. Community from the page. The master page's Statistics and For Sale boxes, read by label like _dgLiveStats() reads a release's (no request). Then the data quality from the answer.
  5. Two columns. A facts card can take a second list of rows: two dls in a grid that is one column below about 360 px. Both Community cards use it.
  6. Before the build, a probe. About 15 masters of other shapes, anonymous, as the release probe did: a CD master ("1-1"), a box set master, a single, one without a tracklist, one with sub-tracks. The shipped splitter then runs on the saved answers.
  7. Tests and docs. A fixture spec on master 26725 plus two probed masters; the master page's two boxes trimmed from debug/discogs-master.html into a fixture; a mutation list; docs/claude/discogs.md, HELP, the coverage registry, WIP changelog entries (WIP.1 the master's Tracks view, WIP.2 the two-column Community card).

Questions for you

  1. The right-hand Details column: Credits by person? The release has Identifiers there; a master has none. Drawn: who did what, on which tracks, as Discogs' own Credits box. Or leave the column empty and let the left cards use the width.I recommend Credits by person: it answers "who played on this" at a glance, which the columns answer only track by track.
  2. The number of versions in the head? The master's answer does not have it; one more request (versions page 1) does. Drawn without.I recommend leaving it out: the Versions button's tooltip already tells the count's cost, and Tracks stays one request.
  3. Community: grouped by meaning, or Discogs' order split in half? Drawn grouped: the collection left, the market right, so Data quality moves up.I recommend grouped.
  4. Videos as a sub-table under the tracks? On a release they are on the second button; on a master the second button is the versions.I recommend under the tracks, as drawn.
