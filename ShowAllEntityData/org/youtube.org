* Mockup (approved design)

- Link: https://claude.ai/artifact/GCrnEVLNyDN6gRG259LbUE
- File: =org/youtube-embeds-mockups.html= (republish from this path to keep the link)
- Look: "Liner notes" (~/CLAUDE.md). Build exactly what the mockup shows plus
  the answers below; anything else is an open question to raise, not to add.

What the mockup shows:
- A YouTube link stays a link with its preview card and window exactly as
  now (the =youtube= reader of =_EXT_READERS=). Beside it a *tile*: a still
  frame (or plain frame) with a ▶, the title, "Play here · 4:30".
- A click (or Enter, or a tap) on the tile plays the video *in place* in
  YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (=youtube-nocookie.com=); nothing is asked
  from YouTube's player host before that click.
- Where the player opens: in a table, a full-width row under the row
  (=tr.playerrow > td[colspan]=); in the Discogs window's Videos section, a
  slot above the list (the list does not jump); elsewhere right under the tile.
- Under the player: a pill (Playing / Unavailable), "Video <id>", "Open on
  YouTube ↗", "■ Stop Esc".
- Esc stops the player first; the next Esc closes the card/window as now.
- The link's window (Space) gets the same tile, playing in the window.
- A video YouTube no longer has shows "This video is not available on YouTube
  any more." with a red *Unavailable* pill (the embed tells us; no oEmbed
  request is needed for that).

* Decisions (vzell, 2026-10-25, answers to the mockup's questions)

1. *Still frames on by default*: each tile shows
   =https://i.ytimg.com/vi/<id>/mqdefault.jpg= (=loading="lazy"=,
   =referrerpolicy="no-referrer"=), so a request to i.ytimg.com per tile as it
   comes into view. A setting turns it off (plain tile, no request at all
   before a click). A playlist has no id-based still: plain tile with "≡ list"
   (no oEmbed request just for a picture) — mention this when building.
2. *MusicBrainz*: the Relationships › URLs sub-table, the sidebar's "Play on
   YouTube" link, and YouTube links inside annotations — all three.
3. *Discogs*: the release window's Videos section, the master window too (the
   master reader reads no =videos= today), and a new "Videos" sub-table on the
   release page — all three.
4. *One player at a time*: starting a second video stops the first.
5. *Size*: as wide as its column, at most 640 px, 16:9 (=aspect-ratio=).
6. *discogs.com*: if the page's frame policy blocks the player, the tile opens
   the video on YouTube in a new tab instead (and says so).
