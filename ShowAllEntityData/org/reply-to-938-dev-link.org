Hi Miodrag - @majkinetor,

in case you'd like to try SAED next to your `codex/interop-938` branch, here is the development version:

**[Install ShowAllEntityData (development version)](https://github.com/vzell/musicbrainz-userscripts/raw/main/ShowAllEntityData/ShowAllEntityData.user.js)**

A few notes:

- This is much newer than the published release (9.99.1167, the one your #926 review looked at): link previews, the other sites (Discogs, setlist.fm, archive.org, the Springsteen sites), the findings menus and a lot more. It also carries the Event Horizon fixes from this issue.
- Please disable the published SAED while you try it. Two copies on one page get in each other's way.
- It stays on this version until I publish the next release, which then arrives as a normal update.
- A quick tour with String Theory from your branch:
  1. Open a release, e.g. https://musicbrainz.org/release/1d404e1d-fcb6-3a52-b478-e706e893c897, and press **V** for MusicBrainz's page.
  2. Press **Show all Tracks for Release** (SAED's button in the header). ISRC Scout's ISRC and Links columns become sortable and filterable, with 📊 per column.
  3. **V** switches between EH's view and SAED's table, and the table survives any number of switches. EH's single-letter keys are ignored on MusicBrainz's page; inside EH's view they work as always.
- It already speaks your `codex/interop-938` conventions (`data-mbu-foreign`, `data-ii-platform`, `data-mbu-quiet` and the ⋯ menu items); with String Theory from that branch, EH's ⋯ menu on a release offers "SAED: Show all Tracks for Release". Details in my next comment.

Feedback is very welcome, here or in my repo.

Thanks!
