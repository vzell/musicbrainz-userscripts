What the window's title bar does now
- Glyphs: every step starts with its glyph: MusicBrainz's own entity icon, or the Discogs emoji (with a small Discogs mark for searches, help articles, images and history). The glyph links to that kind's documentation.
- Going back: clicking an earlier step shows it again from memory, with no new request, and drops the steps after it. The "…" button opens a menu of the whole path, and any step there can be clicked.
- Cards: resting on a step shows that step's own card, with no Ctrl and no request; Space on it goes back there. Resting on a glyph shows the documentation page's card, and clicking it reads the page in the window.
- Width: a narrow window (under 620 px) keeps two steps instead of three.
- New setting: each step shows the entity's name by default, or its kind. A view (an editing history, a documentation page, a Discogs search) always shows its kind.
- MusicBrainz glyph on discogs.com: the first part of item 3 is fixed. The glyph now loads from musicbrainz.org instead of discogs.com.
