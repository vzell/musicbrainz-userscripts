In the Discogs interface, specific glyphs and visual prefixes are used to uniquely identify and link database entities in search results, forums, release pages, and marketplace listings.
The primary entity icons and their underlying database formatting codes include:

💿 Master Releases

• Icon: A small, transparent layered vinyl stack or folder symbol.
• Meaning: Indicates a Database Master Release. This functions as a master folder grouping multiple physical releases, versions, reissues, or promotional pressings of the same overall album, EP, or single together.
• Text Formatting Code: Entered as [m=Master ID] or [mID] in forums and release notes.

📄 Releases

• Icon: A single vinyl record or compact disc silhouette (often looking like a flat disc with a center ring).
• Meaning: Represents a specific, standalone physical variation or catalog printing of an album.
• Text Formatting Code: Entered as [r=Release ID] or [rID].

🏷️ Labels

• Icon: A vintage record label tag or a stylized graphic resembling a record spindle hole with text lines.
• Meaning: Represents a record label, production company, distributor, or pressing plant profile page.
• Text Formatting Code: Entered as [l=Label Name] or [lID].

👤 Artists

• Icon: A standard single person bust / silhouette avatar profile.
• Meaning: Directs to the profile page of a specific band, solo artist, producer, or mastering engineer.
• Text Formatting Code: Entered as [a=Artist Name] or [aID].

💬 Users

• Icon: A outlined person portrait silhouette or a standard comment bubble depending on if it's pointing to a profile or a message board.
• Meaning: Represents a member of the Discogs community, typically an online collector, buyer, seller, or database contributor.
• Text Formatting Code: Entered as [u=Username].
